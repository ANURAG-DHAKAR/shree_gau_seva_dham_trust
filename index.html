About Our Trust

Serving Cows, Serving Humanity

Shree Gau Seva Dham Trust is a registered charitable trust dedicated to the protection, care, and welfare of cows. Our gaushala provides a safe haven for abandoned, injured, and old cows, while also promoting awareness about the importance of cow protection in our society.

Our History (हमारा इतिहास)

Shree Gau Seva Dham Trust was established in 2010 with a vision to protect and serve cows. What started as a small initiative has now grown into a dedicated gaushala providing care and shelter to hundreds of cows.

Our Work

Gau Seva Activities

Dedicated comprehensive care and protection to every single cow.

Daily Feeding

Providing fresh green fodder, grains, jaggery, and clean drinking water to all cows twice daily.

Medical Care

Regular health checkups, emergency surgeries, vaccinations, and round-the-clock veterinary doctors on call.

Rescue & Rehabilitation

Dedicated ambulance service to rescue accident-injured, sick, or abandoned stray cows from roads.

Shelter & Cleanliness

Spacious, hygienic, climate-controlled sheds ensuring safety during harsh winters and summers.

Calves & Senior Care

Special nutrition and gentle care for newly born calves and non-milking aged senior cows.

Organic Bio-Energy

Utilizing cow dung for vermicompost and bio-gas generation promoting sustainable eco-friendly farming.
